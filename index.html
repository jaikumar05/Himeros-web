About Us

Driven by Innovation, Dedicated to Life

Himeros Pharma was established in 2019 with a vision to contribute to the healthcare and pharmaceutical sector through quality-focused business solutions and professional excellence. Based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, the company was founded with the objective of supporting the growing healthcare ecosystem by delivering reliable and customer-centric pharmaceutical services.

At Himeros Pharma, we believe that innovation, integrity, and commitment form the foundation of sustainable growth. Our team strives to maintain high standards of professionalism while building strong relationships with partners, healthcare professionals, and stakeholders.
