About This Book

Published by Tulika Collections — the publishing arm of Himeros Pharma — this first-edition guide addresses a critical need in clinical practice: a structured, practical, and accessible resource for healthcare professionals actively involved in sexual health management and sex therapy.

The book bridges the gap between clinical theory and real-world practice, providing comprehensive guidance on patient assessment, therapeutic modalities, and evidence-based interventions across a spectrum of sexual health concerns. It draws on established psychological, behavioural, and medical frameworks to deliver a holistic approach to sex therapy.

Whether used as a primary reference or a supplementary clinical tool, this guide empowers clinicians to approach sexual health conversations and therapeutic sessions with confidence, compassion, and clinical rigour.

What Makes This Guide Indispensable

Patient-Centred Approach

Guides clinicians in conducting sensitive, patient-focused sexual health consultations that build trust and encourage open communication.

Therapeutic Modalities

Covers CBT, sensate focus, mindfulness-based approaches, and integrative techniques — all translated into actionable clinical steps.

Assessment Frameworks

Structured templates and validated assessment tools for evaluating sexual dysfunction and guiding personalised treatment planning.

Couples & Individual Therapy

Addresses both individual and partner-based sexual health concerns, with specific guidance for couples therapy contexts.

Ethical & Inclusive

Grounded in ethical clinical practice with guidance on cultural sensitivity, inclusivity, and professional boundaries in sex therapy.

Evidence-Based Foundation

All clinical recommendations are underpinned by contemporary research and validated practices in sexual medicine and psychosexual therapy.
